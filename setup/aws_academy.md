# AWS Academy

You will receive an email invitation to join the course in AWS Academy. Follow the steps below that match your situation.

**Note:** AWS Academy runs on its own instance of Canvas. It is separate from UVA Canvas, and your UVA Canvas login will not work there.

### If you do not have an AWS Academy account yet

1. Open the invitation email and click **Get Started**.
2. Click **Create My Account**. Do not choose the option to log in with an existing Canvas account; that refers to an AWS Academy account, not your UVA Canvas account.
3. Enter a password and click **Register**.

### If you already have an AWS Academy account

1. Open the invitation email and click **Get Started**.
2. Log in to AWS Academy if you are not already logged in.
3. When asked to confirm that you want to join the course, click **Yes**.
